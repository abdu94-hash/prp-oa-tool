Product characterization and dose calculation

The absolute platelet dose is the compositional descriptor most closely corresponding to what the joint receives, and it is the one least often recorded. In one survey of practicing physicians, complete blood counts were not ordered in 93.3% of cases — meaning the dose was unknown.

How the dose is calculatedAbsolute platelet dose (×109) = product concentration (×109/L) × injected volume (mL) ÷ 1000. The 10×109 reference is a hypothesis proposed in the dose literature, not an established standard; at least one characterized cohort found non-responders receiving a higher dose than responders. Use the calculation to know what you are delivering, not to assume that more is better.

Evidence explorer

Every entry is a real, verified PubMed record. Filter by joint, study type, comparator, or direction of finding. Direction describes what the study reported, not an endorsement.

Read the comparator column.Superiority over hyaluronic acid is a weak claim, because hyaluronic acid performs close to placebo in large blinded trials. Intra-articular saline is not inert either: it exceeds the minimal clinically important difference for WOMAC at six months. The strongest designs are those blinding participant, injector and assessor against a placebo control.
